Transatlantic Differences

Few quick examples of differences from experience in the industrial sector:

Price Negotiation & Levels

Rarely a price negotiation in the US compared to Europe/Asia. However, prices for industrial supplies are often 30-50% higher in the US due to distributor networks.

Engineering & Design

Designs differ due to culture, usage, and available raw materials (metric vs. imperial). Subcontractors can be more expensive in the US, requiring more in-house adaptation.

Market Speed & Support

Decisions are faster in the larger US market. Expectations for customer service are high; customers are supportive but demand strong backing for products.

Experiences & Advices

Having stepped back, I now mentor pro bono for early conversations. The purpose is to save founders the time it took me to learn.

Shop-Floor Reality

Insight comes from working directly alongside teams. To truly understand a job, spend time with the people doing it. Listening is learning, doing is understanding.

Customer Proximity

Active listening and building trusted relationships. Visiting customers and spending time on their floor to talk to operators and maintenance teams brings invaluable advice.

People & Humility

Lean, motivated teams with mutual respect outperform heavy structures. Empower people with tools and responsibility. Titles don't define value.

Long-Term Vision

Sustainable value over short-term profit chasing. Acquiring new customers requires investment, while losing an existing one happens quickly. Beyond the financial impact, it also damages your reputation.

Organization

Team Dynamics: Having top-tier players isn't enough; if they don't mesh well together, the collective output falls far short of a cohesive, motivated team. Building a strong team takes time and intentional effort.

Diverse Cultures

Perspective: Methods of working and thinking can vary significantly across cultures. Instead of judging differences, strive to understand them and incorporate valuable ideas. Embracing cultural diversity is a tremendous opportunity to broaden your horizons, both personally and professionally.

Resilience & Ambition

Embracing Failure: Mistakes happen—it's completely normal. Taking action and trying is the only way to succeed. Having a clear goal and strong motivation is essential to stay on track.

The Formula: risk + goal = success

Investment Focus

Depending on the company and the opportunity, I am also open to investing alongside providing strategic and operational support.

I am particularly interested in niche industrial products, especially businesses combining mechanical, electrical, automation, or other technical components.

Investment Philosophy

"Il faut se tromper pour apprendre."

Embracing calculated risks and learning from failure is essential. A sound investment isn't just about capital, but about navigating mistakes to build sustainable, long-term success.
